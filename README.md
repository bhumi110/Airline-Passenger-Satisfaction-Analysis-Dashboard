# Airline Passenger Satisfaction Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes airline passenger satisfaction, service quality, flight delays, and customer segments to identify areas where the airline can improve passenger experience.

## Business Problem

Airlines collect large amounts of passenger feedback, but raw survey data makes it difficult to identify the main areas of dissatisfaction. This project analyzes passenger survey data to understand satisfaction patterns, evaluate service performance, and identify customer segments and operational factors that require attention.

## Objective

- Measure overall passenger satisfaction and dissatisfaction
- Identify the highest and lowest-rated airline services
- Analyze the relationship between flight delays and satisfaction
- Compare satisfaction across travel classes and customer types
- Identify passenger segments that require greater attention
- Provide data-driven recommendations for improving customer experience

## Dataset

The dataset contains **103,904 passenger records** with information including:

- Passenger demographics
- Customer loyalty
- Travel class and travel type
- Flight distance
- Departure and arrival delays
- Airline service ratings
- Overall passenger satisfaction

## Dashboard

The dashboard is divided into four analytical pages:

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides an overall view of passenger satisfaction using KPIs and visuals for travel class, customer type, flight distance, and delay categories.

### 2. Service Quality Analysis
Compares passenger ratings across services such as inflight service, baggage handling, seat comfort, online booking, cleanliness, food and drink, and inflight Wi-Fi.

### 3. Delay & Flight Experience
Analyzes departure and arrival delays, delayed vs. on-time passengers, travel class, and flight distance to understand operational performance.

### 4. Passenger & Segment Analysis
Examines satisfaction across customer loyalty, gender, age groups, travel type, and other passenger segments.

## Key Insights

- Overall satisfaction is **43.33%**, while **56.67%** of passengers are dissatisfied.
- **Inflight Service** and **Baggage Handling** are among the highest-rated services at approximately **3.6/5**.
- **Inflight Wi-Fi** is the lowest-rated service at approximately **2.7/5**, followed by **Ease of Online Booking** at approximately **2.8/5**.
- Satisfaction generally decreases as flight delays increase, from **45.75% for on-time passengers** to around **36% for longer delays**.
- Business-class passengers show substantially higher satisfaction than Economy and Eco Plus passengers.
- **81.73%** of passengers are classified as loyal customers, but only **47.73% of loyal customers are satisfied**.
- Disloyal passengers have a much lower satisfaction rate, with only **23.67% satisfied**.
- Business travelers generally show higher satisfaction than personal travelers across the analyzed age groups.

## Business Recommendations

The analysis suggests that the airline should prioritize improvements in low-rated services such as **inflight Wi-Fi and online booking**, focus on reducing and managing flight delays, investigate the satisfaction gap between travel classes, and target dissatisfied disloyal passengers through customer retention and service-recovery initiatives.
